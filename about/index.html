Haps pulls together events from venues, ticketing platforms, and public calendars across your area — so finding something to do is as easy as a swipe.

How Haps works

  1. 1

    Swipe through what’s on

    Swipe right to save an event, left to pass. Browsing plans should feel fun, not like homework.

  2. 2

    Saving just means “remember this”

    A saved event is a private bookmark so you don’t forget it’s happening — not a ticket, not an RSVP, and nobody gets notified. It’s simply your shortlist of things you might want to do.

  3. 3

    Show up if you feel like it

    When the day comes, check your saved list, grab tickets from the venue if you need them, and go — or don’t. Haps just makes sure it was on your radar.
