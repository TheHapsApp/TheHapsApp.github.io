# Delete Your Haps Account

_Last updated: 2026-06-04_

This page explains how to delete your **Haps** account (the event-discovery app, package `org.rustdev.haps`) and the data associated with it. You can delete your account directly in the app, or request deletion by email if you no longer have the app installed.

## Option 1 — Delete in the app

1. Open **Haps** and go to **Settings** (the gear icon in the bottom navigation).
2. In the **Account** section, tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm on the *"Delete your account?"* prompt.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately, and you are signed out. This option appears once you have signed in with Google or Apple.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email **admin@thehaps.app** from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line **"Delete my account."** We verify ownership of the address and delete the account and its data **within 30 days** (usually much sooner). There is no charge.

## What gets deleted

- Your account and profile (email address, display name, and profile picture obtained from Google/Apple sign-in)
- Your saved events, dismissed events, and check-ins
- Any events you submitted through the app
- Your authentication records on our backend

## What is kept

- **Anonymous usage analytics** (e.g. app opens, screens viewed) are **de-identified** — the link to your account is permanently removed so the remaining records can no longer be associated with you. They are never tied back to your identity. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
- We retain information only where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements, and only for as long as the law requires.

## Questions

For anything related to account or data deletion, contact **admin@thehaps.app**.
